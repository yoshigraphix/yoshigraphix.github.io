How to play

First horse past the post after the last lap wins. Every turn you take a chance — the trick is knowing when to stop.

Roll the hoof die. Faces 1–5 move you; the horseshoe is a stumble.

Push your luck. After a roll, stop — or push and roll again, adding it up. Pushes cost 1, then 2, then 3… stamina. Stumble and you keep only half.

Ease up instead of rolling: move 1, get +3 stamina and draw a card. At 0 stamina you can’t push.

Pick a lane after you roll. Bends have 8 spaces on the rail, 11 on the outside. One lane over is free; two lanes costs 1 stamina.

No passing through. A rider in your lane boxes you in — you stop behind them. But tucking in right behind someone is a slipstream: +1 stamina.

Comebacks: each round the rider at the back goes first, and whoever is last draws a card at the start of their turn. Crossing the line each lap draws one too.

Computer riders

Nell (Cautious) banks small rolls and loves a slipstream. Rory (Bold) pushes the die hard. Vix (Sly) spooks the leader and parks in your lane to box you in.
