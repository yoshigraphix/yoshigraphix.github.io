Stall Tactics

Every horse, the right stall

How to play

Drag a horse into a stall. Or tap the horse, then tap the stall.

Every horse has wishes. Green means happy. Red means broken, and the stalls light up red.

Fill every stall with every wish green. Stuck? 💡 Hint puts one horse in its right stall.
