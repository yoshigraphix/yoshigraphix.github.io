Ride a dressage test to the music. Tap on the beat, change gait at the letters and win a ribbon from the judges.

How to play

Tap on the beat as each hoofprint reaches the ring. Walk has 4 beats, trot 2, canter 3 (da-da-DUM).

Swipe up to speed up, down to slow down or halt, right when the arrow reaches the ring at the letter.

The judges score every movement from 0 to 10. Ribbons at 55% (yellow), 68% (red) and 80% (blue).
