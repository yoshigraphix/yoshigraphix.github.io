A foal has wandered into the corn maze. Follow the whinny, find the little one and lead them back out the gate before the sun goes down.

How to play

Listen for the whinny. It gets louder as you get close, and comes from the left or right. Ripples at the edge of the screen point the way too.

Tap a path to walk there, or press and drag to steer. Pick up horseshoes and apples for bonus Trotties.

Lead the foal out the gate before the sun sets. At dusk the scarecrow lights a lantern by the gate to show the way.

Paused

The sun waits for you.
