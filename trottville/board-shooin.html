How to play

Two ranches, five foals, one meadow. About 5 minutes.

1 · Move a horseOn your turn move one of your horses 1 or 2 squares in a straight line, any of 8 ways, over empty grass.
2 · Shoo!Every foal right next to where your horse lands bolts straight away until a horse, bale, tree or fence stops it.
3 · Trick shotsA foal running diagonally bounces off the first fence it hits and keeps going.
4 · Into the barnYour barn is across the meadow. A foal that runs out through its door is home. Careful: foals can run into your rival's barn too!

First barn to 3 foals wins. At sunset (30 moves each) the most foals home wins; if level, the most foals on your barn's half.

Tap a horse to see where it can go. A gold dot scores, a red one helps your rival. Dots that shoo a foal show a preview first: tap again to go.
