Load the feed cart at the store, then drive it home. Bumps shake the load, so stack it well and slow down when it matters.

How to play

Drag each delivery onto the cart. Stack it low and snug. Things hanging over the edge fall first.

Hold to trot faster. Let go to slow down before bumps, holes and the bridge.

Reach the farm gate. You score what is still on the cart, plus a bonus for a tidy stack and a quick trip.
