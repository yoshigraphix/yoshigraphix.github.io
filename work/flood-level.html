Behind the experiment

Real terrain. A simplified question.

The prototype converts an elevation grid into a rotatable 3D surface and clips a horizontal water plane against each terrain triangle. The white marker is the supplied center; the perimeter traces the requested half-mile radius. Heights are visually exaggerated 3× to make terrain easier to read.

What this version can tell you

How a chosen horizontal elevation compares with sampled ground. It cannot tell you how many feet the actual river must rise to flood your house. That requires an observed river reference, compatible vertical datums, surveyed building elevations, and hydraulic modeling.

Known limits

No river connectivity, flow, drainage, culverts, levees, buildings, rainfall, or flood timing. Terrain is sampled about every 20 meters; source resolution, age, and vertical accuracy vary. The circular mesh boundary is approximate. Do not use this for safety, insurance, or evacuation decisions.
