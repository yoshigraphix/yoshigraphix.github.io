The Client

Tynker

Tynker is an educational platform that teaches coding to kids through interactive games and activities, making learning to program fun and engaging. Children develop essential STEM skills while exploring their creativity in a familiar gaming environment.

The Project

Crafting the Mythicraft sizzle reel was a comprehensive project that drew on my whole skill set. I went into the virtual landscapes of Minecraft to capture in-game footage showcasing Tynker's interactive coding lessons, then curated audio elements to carry the storytelling — a seamless fusion of visuals and sound.

I also designed and illustrated the custom graphics that adorn the reel. Each frame was built with intricate detail and vibrant color, inviting viewers into the work.

  • Sizzle reel
  • Strong marketing asset
