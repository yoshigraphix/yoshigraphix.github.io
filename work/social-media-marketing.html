The Client

Jackbox Games & Tynker

Jackbox Games is a leading game studio known for the Jackbox Party Pack series — party games played with smartphones as controllers. Tynker is an educational tech company teaching kids to code through interactive courses, building early interest in computer science and STEM.

The Project

At Tynker I crafted graphics showcasing the platform's educational programming offerings — game design, web design, animation, robotics. These images were the visual argument for Tynker's mission: helping K-12 learners pick up coding in a way that actually holds their attention.

At Jackbox Games I developed marketing images that captured the essence of the games and spoke to their audience. Infusing humor and wit into the visuals produced content that entertained first and converted second. Placed across social, email, and banners, these images communicated what made each platform worth trying.
